How to read this map

  • E Evidence: Stated or observed in the provided research
  • I Interpretation: Inferred from cited evidence
  • A Assumption: No evidence yet; hypothesis to validate
  • P Proposed: Design idea; not a validated outcome
  • ★ Moment of truth: the customer decides to continue, trust, pay or give up. These columns are tinted amber.
  • Emotion: −2 to +2. Dashed line and hollow dot = assumed or proposed.
