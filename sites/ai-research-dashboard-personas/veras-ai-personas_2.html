    Churn Survey

    AI insights from cancellations · 1 Jun – 31 Aug 2026 · all Chaos products

    The survey covers every product. The AI findings below come from small subsets, and each figure names its own base: Veras cancellations (1,772), the AI follow-up question (320), and comments that mention AI (291) or credits (518).

    49.8%of Veras cancellers who saw no value couldn't achieve the results they wanted (n=211; 25.0% across products)
    35.1%of Veras customers who switched moved to an AI tool, the top alternative (26 of 74)
    56.4%of Veras cancellers never started or used it only once or twice (n=1,772; 37.9% across products)
    60.3%of those saying AI reduced their need report it replaced some or most of their work (n=320)

    Method and caveats

    Sources, sample sizes, limits

    Sources

    • CG Polls Analysis: eight single-question community polls, 163 to 514 responses each, segmented by self-reported role.
    • Preliminary insights, Veras research (July 2026): 19 interviews plus 2 email feedbacks.
    • Customer research on asset workflows & AI (21–30 April 2025): 3 interviews, shown by initials.
    • CAB Insights: Customer Advisory Board ratings of 12 AI features (importance and excitement, 1–5, n=14) and the AI Ideation Workshop in Miro. Workshop points are paraphrased.
    • Churn Survey raw export (1 Jun – 31 Aug 2026, all Chaos products), including 1,772 Veras cancellations. AI-specific answers are small subsets: 320 on how AI changed their workflow, 291 comments mentioning AI, 518 mentioning credits. Comment themes are keyword-coded.
    • CSAT survey: 15,000+ responses across 2 iterations and all products, including verbatims (anonymous, shown with product tier) and feature-interest results (Solo n=453, Tiers n=458).
    • Veras Research Explorer: the workflow process map, participant profiles and quote bank from 18 analysed interviews.

    Caveats

    • Polls were answered by different people, so 2,277 is total responses, not unique respondents.
    • Small role samples are directional: Studio Owners (n=2 to 17), Students (n=10 to 41), Freelancers (n=10 to 54).
    • A few role rows in the source tables sum slightly above 100%. Values are shown as reported.
    • For tutorials, the source lists only each role's top topics; missing cells show as “n/r”.

    About the personas

    • Names and syntheses are interpretive labels over the poll data. The source also calls the Design Author the “Design Director”.
    • No students were interviewed. Emerging Creator quotes come from interviewees new to rendering or to AI.
    • In Compare, two-role personas use their first role: Visual Creator = Visualization Artist, Workflow Pragmatist = Freelancer.
