Model view drops the substitutions and shows only the platforms that actually carry the model you pick.

Method. Pure AI = cheapest subscription whose value is substantially the allowance itself, which excludes bundles like Enscape where the seat buys CAD-integrated rendering and credits ride along. Entry = cheapest paid tier. Top-up = marginal rate after the allowance is spent. PromeAI publishes no plan prices and falls back to its top-up rate at every basis. Platforms that sell no top-up fall back to their plan rate on the Top-up basis.

— No vendor bills in tokens. Chaos, xFigura, Firefly and Runway bill in credits; PromeAI in coins; Krea in compute units; Midjourney in Fast GPU-minutes. All are normalized to $/unit here.

— Veras is resolution-graduated, not flat. NB 2 Image runs 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 credits across 0.5k / 1k / 2k / 4k, and Grok Image runs 5 (Fast) / 10 (1k) / 15 (2k). What collapses is the model ladder at 2K, where Grok, NB 2 and NB Pro all cost 15. The flat ones are the competing budget models: Firefly’s FLUX.2 klein is 5 credits at any resolution, xFigura’s Flux 2 Klein is 1.

— A platform is listed only when it can actually produce the configuration selected. It is hidden when it does not carry the model, does not offer the resolution, or the clip length falls outside the durations it offers. A resolution nothing offers is greyed out in the control above.

— Greyed costs marked “extrapolated from per-second cost” fall inside a platform’s duration range but on a length it does not list, so the figure is its per-second rate times the clip length rather than a price the product quotes. Hover the note to see which lengths it does offer.

— Chaos is the only vendor in this set publishing a sub-1K image tier, and Seedream 5 is its only model with a 3K tier.

— Quality tier names are not aligned across platforms. Firefly’s Low/Medium/High and Veras’ Medium/High or Fast/High/Extra High/Max are each vendor’s own labels, and nothing published maps one to another. The default prices every platform at the setting its figures were quoted at; picking a named quality scales only the platforms that declare it.

— xFigura figures reflect in-app estimates as of 21 Sep 2026.
